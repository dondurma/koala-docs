# Bill Import Tips

Before importing bills, please read the following to avoid interruptions or incomplete data.

## Don't import too many at once

- We recommend importing **no more than 1000 records** at a time. If you have more, import them in **batches** (for example, 500–1000 each time).
- Free users have a total limit of **300 bills** across all ledgers; members have no limit. If you have a lot of data, check your remaining quota before importing.

## Be patient with large files

- Parsing and importing both take time. The more records, the longer it takes — near or above 1000 records, it may take a while (tens of seconds or more).
- Don't assume the app has frozen; simply wait for the progress to finish.

## Don't leave the page while importing

- While importing, please **stay on the current page**: don't go back, switch to another app, lock the screen, or force-close the app.
- Leaving midway may **interrupt the import**, so only part of the data is imported, or you may need to import again.

## After importing

- Once the import finishes, we recommend syncing once manually to make sure your data is uploaded.
- Then check a few bills in the list to confirm the amount, category, and date are correct.
