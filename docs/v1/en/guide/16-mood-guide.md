# Mood Check-in Guide

## 📋 Overview

Expense Tracker: Koala (“Koala”) provides a “Mood card” on the Overview page, so you can quickly record your mood each day while tracking expenses and review it in a monthly view. It’s a good fit if you want to keep an eye on both your spending and how you feel, or if you want to build a daily check-in habit.

---

## 🚀 How to Record Your Mood

1. Open Expense Tracker: Koala and go to the **“Overview”** page in the bottom navigation bar
2. Find the **“Mood card”** (for the current month, if you haven’t recorded today yet, three options appear at the bottom of the card)
3. Tap **Happy / Neutral / Sad** to record today’s mood

> 📝 **Note**:
> - You can only record once per day (based on the calendar day)
> - Once today’s mood is recorded, the options no longer appear that day, and it **cannot be edited or deleted inside the app**
> - You can only record the mood for “today”; backfilling or editing past dates is not supported

---

## 📅 View the Monthly Calendar

The mood card shows the current month’s records:

- The top shows the **number of recorded days / total days** for the month, plus today’s mood
- Below, a grid shows each day of the month: recorded days are colored by mood (one color each for Happy / Neutral / Sad), and unrecorded days are shown in a light color
- Future dates are not shown

> 💡 **Tip**: When you switch the Overview page to another month, the card shows that month’s records (without the “today” information).

---

## 🎨 Personalization

Go to **“Settings” → “Personalization”** to adjust mood-related options:

- **Show mood card**: toggle whether the mood card appears on the Overview page (shown by default)
- **Mood colors**: customize the colors for the three moods (Happy / Neutral / Sad)

> 📝 **Note**: The mood colors also apply to the heatmap generated by “Statistics page → Share → Mood records”.

---

## 📤 Share Mood Records

1. Open the **“Statistics”** page in the bottom navigation bar
2. At the bottom of the page, tap **“Share this page >”**
3. In the menu that appears, choose **“Mood records”**
4. After the mood heatmap is generated, send or save it via the system share sheet

> ⚠️ **Note**:
> - “Mood records” sharing is only available when the time dimension is “Monthly” or “Yearly” (the option is not shown for a custom time range)
> - Mood records are independent of bill data and do not affect bill statistics

---

## 🔄 Multi-Device Sync

- **After signing in**: mood records sync with your account — sign in with the same account on multiple devices and you will see the same mood records
- **When not signed in**: mood records are only stored on this device, and cannot be recovered after uninstalling or switching devices

> 📝 Mood records sync by account and are independent of ledger data.

---

## 🔧 FAQ

### Q1: I recorded the wrong mood — can I change it?

You can only record once per day, and after recording it cannot be edited or deleted inside the app. Please confirm before recording.

### Q2: Can I backfill the mood for the past few days?

Currently you can only record the mood for “today”; backfilling or editing past dates is not supported.

### Q3: Why can’t I see the mood card on the Overview page?

Please check whether **“Settings” → “Personalization” → “Show mood card”** is enabled.

### Q4: Are my mood records still there after switching devices?

If you are signed in and have completed a sync, reinstalling and signing in with the same account restores them via account sync; if you are not signed in, the data is stored only on this device.
