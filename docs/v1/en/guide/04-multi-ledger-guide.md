# Multi-ledger

## 📋 Overview

Koala supports multiple ledgers so you can manage different budgets separately. Each ledger has its own name, currency, categories, and statistics.

Common scenarios:

- Personal
- Family
- Travel
- Baby
- Car
- Home renovation
- Business

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Create a new ledger

1. Open Koala
2. Tap **Settings**
3. Tap **Ledger management**
4. Tap **+**
5. Fill in ledger info (name/description/icon/currency)
6. Tap **Save**

### Switch ledgers

1. Open **Ledger management**
2. Tap a ledger card to switch

> 📝 Note: After switching, overview/statistics/bill list will show data for the current ledger.

---

## 📚 Ledger management

### Edit a ledger

1. Open **Ledger management**
2. Tap the **More** button on the ledger card
3. Edit name/description/icon/currency
4. Tap **Save**

### Delete a ledger

1. Open ledger details
2. Tap **Delete ledger**
3. Confirm

> ⚠️ Warning:
> - Deleting a ledger removes the ledger and its bills.
> - The ledger is removed from this device; if cloud sync is enabled, it will also be removed from the cloud after the next sync.
> - This action cannot be restored inside the app.

---

## 🔧 FAQ

### How many ledgers can I create?

You can create up to **50** ledgers.

### Are accounts ledger-based?

No. Accounts are device-based and shared across all ledgers.
