# Budget Guide

## 📋 Overview

Budgets are a **membership feature**. You can set one total budget for a month, and add separate budgets for specific categories, so the Overview page tells you how much is left.

## 🚀 Where to find it

Go to **Settings → Budget Management**.

> 📝 Non-members see a "membership feature" prompt; subscribing unlocks it.

## 💰 Set a monthly budget

1. Tap the **pencil** next to "Monthly budget"
2. Enter the amount on the calculator and tap Done
3. Enter 0 to clear it — you'll be asked to confirm "Clear this month's budget"

> 📝 The maximum is 9,999,999.99. Budgets are stored per month, so months don't affect each other.

## 🗂 Set category budgets

1. Tap "Add category budget"
2. Pick **top-level** or **subcategory**, then choose a category
3. Enter the amount and confirm

Rules:

- Only **one level** per category tree: if "Food" already has a budget, its subcategories can't have one (and vice versa) — the app tells you when it conflicts.
- Each category row shows "spent / budget" with a progress bar; tap it to see that category's bills for the month.

## 📊 Overview progress bar

Under the "Monthly summary" card you'll see the budget amount, percentage, and a progress bar:

- **Below 50%**: safe color
- **50%–100%**: warning color
- **Above 100%**: overspend color, labeled "Over budget"

Tap it to jump straight to that month's budget page. All three colors can be customized in budget settings.

## 🔁 Carry-over and overspend deduction

Open **Settings → Budget Management → slider icon (top right)**. Four switches:

- **Budget switch**: hides the progress bar on Overview (data is kept)
- **Include category budgets in monthly budget**: adds category budgets to the total, changing amount and percentage
- **Carry over remaining balance**: unused money moves to next month
- **Deduct overspend from next month**: overspent money is taken from next month

> 📝 Only **settled months** actually pass their balance forward. The current month only shows an estimate such as "Remaining ¥xx · transfers after month-end".
> 📝 The carry-over start is the month you **first turned the switch on**; earlier months are not backfilled.

## 📱 Multiple devices

Once signed in, budgets sync with your account:

- Automatically after reopening the app and while you stay on the Overview page — no manual refresh needed
- Budget is a Pro feature and is unavailable when you are not signed in

## 🔧 FAQ

### Q1: Why is there no progress bar on Overview?

Check three things: the budget switch is on, the month has a budget, and the account is a member. Any missing item hides the bar.

### Q2: I changed one month's budget — why didn't the others change?

Budgets are stored per month; set each month separately.

### Q3: Why doesn't carry-over include older months?

The start point is fixed to the month you first enabled carry-over. To accumulate from earlier, you'd have to start earlier.

### Q4: After deleting a category, is its budget gone?

Yes — deleting a category clears its budget, and both devices stop showing it after syncing.
